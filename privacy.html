Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dengernih Uploader ("the App"), operated by Dengernih, handles information. The App is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the Dengernih channel to upload video clips to Dengernih's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

1. Information the App accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos and set video metadata (title, description, tags, privacy status, schedule) on the authorized YouTube channel, and basic channel information needed to confirm which channel is connected.
  • TikTok (via TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API): basic account information (such as display name and open ID) and permission to upload or post videos to the authorized TikTok account.
  • Authorization tokens: OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google and TikTok after the account owner grants permission.

2. How information is used

The information is used only to upload Dengernih's own video clips and their metadata to Dengernih's own accounts, and to check upload status. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored locally on the channel owner's personal computer and are never sent to any server operated by Dengernih. The App has no backend server and no database of users.

4. Sharing

Dengernih does not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with Google/YouTube and TikTok as needed to perform uploads.

5. Third-party services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The App's use of TikTok services is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page or in TikTok's app settings under Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions. Deleting the token files from the owner's computer removes all locally stored data. To request deletion or ask a question, contact us at asfaahamd2003@gmail.com.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than the channel owner.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
